📊 5-Pillar Scoring System

💰 Cash Flow
Weight: 25% (30% in NTC)
Score: 0-100
Cash ratio, inflows, bounces, balance
📋 Compliance
Weight: 20% (24% in NTC)
Score: 0-100
GST filing, EPFO, tax ratio
📈 Growth
Weight: 20% (23% in NTC)
Score: 0-100
Revenue growth, employee growth, trend
🛡️ Stability
Weight: 20% (23% in NTC)
Score: 0-100
Revenue consistency, buyer diversity, age
💳 Credit Worthiness
Weight: 15% (NULL in NTC)
Score: 0-100
CIBIL score, DPD penalties

🌱 New-to-Credit (NTC) Fairness

Problem: Traditional models reject businesses without CIBIL history

Solution: NTC Mode redistributes credit's 15% weight to alternate-data pillars

Standard Mode (with CIBIL):
Overall = Cash Flow(25%) + Compliance(20%) + Growth(20%) + Stability(20%) + Credit(15%)
NTC Mode (no CIBIL):
Overall = Cash Flow(30%) + Compliance(24%) + Growth(23%) + Stability(23%) // Credit pillar → NULL, weight distributed to GST/UPI/EPFO

Result: ~40% of MSMEs with no CIBIL now get fair scores instead of automatic rejection

👥 Two Roles & Complete Flows

👤 Business Owner (MSME)

Portal Entry: Landing → AuthPage → MSMEPortal → Result

Step 1: Register/Login with email + password

Step 2: Enter GSTIN, business details, years in business

Step 3: Check "I consent to fetch my financial data"

Step 4: Get instant financial health score (0-100)

Step 5: View 5 pillars, key drivers, loan eligibility

Step 6: Apply for loan or compare with peers

Subsequent Access:

  • View score history (trend line)
  • Re-score same GSTIN (appends to snapshot)
  • View consent artifacts
  • Chat with AI advisor

🏦 Bank Officer (Banker)

Portal Entry: Landing → AuthPage → Dashboard → Applications

Step 1: Register/Login with email + password (role: banker)

Step 2: View portfolio dashboard (all scores, KPIs)

Step 3: See incoming loan applications (table view)

Step 4: Click application → Review full score + explanation

Step 5: Approve/Reject + add notes

Step 6: Post-disbursement: Record outcome (repaid/npa)

Portfolio Analytics:

  • Repayment rate by risk band
  • NPA concentration by city
  • Score distribution (histogram)
  • Loan product performance

Authentication

Score Generation & History

POST /api/score/generate
Input: {gstin, business_name, business_type, city, years_in_business, consent_given}
Output: {pillar_scores, loan_eligibility, explanations, consent_artifact}
GET /api/score/demo
Get a pre-generated demo score (for testing)
GET /api/score/history
Get all scores for authenticated user
GET /api/score/trend?msme_id=...
Get score trend history (append-only snapshots)
GET /api/score/benchmark?business_type=...&city=...
