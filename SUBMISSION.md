# FinHealth AI - IDBI Innovate 2026 Submission

## Executive Summary

FinHealth AI is India's first AI-powered credit scoring platform for MSMEs using alternate data.

**Problem:** 43M Indian MSMEs are "credit invisible" — zero formal financial records.

**Solution:** AI scoring in 30 seconds using GST, bank accounts, UPI.

**Impact:** Unlock 5-10 trillion rupees in credit for underserved MSME sector.

---

## Problem Statement

- 43M MSMEs have no formal financial records
- 8% average NPA rate (poor credit assessment)
- 2-3 weeks for loan approval (manual process)
- MSMEs employ 11M+ people, contribute 30% of GDP

---

## Solution

### For Business Owners
1. Enter GSTIN
2. Get instant score (0-100)
3. See loan eligibility
4. Apply in seconds

### For Bank Managers
1. Dashboard of applications
2. One-click approve/reject
3. SHAP-explained credit report
4. Compliance audit trail

---

## Key Features

✅ 5-Pillar Score (Cash Flow, Compliance, Growth, Stability, Credit)
✅ 11 Loan Products (Mudra, CGTMSE, Business, Working Capital, etc.)
✅ New-to-Credit Fairness (NTC engine for first-time borrowers)
✅ SHAP Explainability (AI transparency)
✅ RBI AA Compliant (Regulatory approved)
✅ 30 Seconds to Score (vs. 14-21 days)
