The Problem

Traditional Banking Fails MSMEs

India ke MSMEs economy ki backbone hain — 45% GDP, 110 million jobs — lekin formal credit access sirf 16% ko milta hai.

❌ Traditional Approach

  • 📄3 saal ka ITR, audited balance sheet mandatory
  • ⏳4-6 week processing time
  • 🔴Credit score nahi = instant rejection (NTC/NTB MSMEs)
  • 👤Loan officer bias, inconsistent evaluation
  • ❓No explanation — sirf "Rejected" milta hai
  • 🏪Rural / semi-urban businesses underserved
  • 📦One-size-fits-all — sector differences ignored

✅ FinHealth AI Approach

  • 🔐GSTIN se data pull — no document upload needed
  • ⚡90-second assessment via AA Framework
  • 🌱NTC mode: credit-invisible MSMEs bhi assess hote hain
  • 🤖ML model — consistent, unbiased, explainable
  • 📊SHAP: "Cash flow kamzor hai, isse 8 points milenge"
  • 🗺️GSTIN se state/city auto-detect, region-aware
  • 🏭8 business types × 10 cities = sector benchmarks

How It Works — End to End

GSTIN daalo se lekar loan offer tak — 90 seconds

1

GSTIN Enter + Consent

MSME apna GSTIN daalta hai. RBI Account Aggregator framework ke through 4 FIPs se consent milta hai: GST Network, NPCI (UPI), EPFO, aur CIBIL. Ek consent artifact generate hota hai — legally valid, digitally signed.

SHA-256 GSTIN seed → deterministic profiling
2

Multi-Source Data Pull

GST returns (12 months), UPI/bank statements, EPFO contribution history, aur CIBIL credit report — 4 sources se 23 features extract hote hain automatically. Koi document upload nahi.

23 features · 4 data sources · deterministic seeded RNG
3

5-Pillar Score Computation

Cash Flow (25%), Compliance (20%), Growth (20%), Stability (20%), Credit (15%) — weighted composite score. NTC mode mein credit pillar suppress hota hai aur weights redistribute hote hain [30/24/23/23].

XGBoost · CREDITWORTHY / RISKY classification
4

SHAP Explainability

TreeExplainer se har feature ka contribution dikhta hai — "GST compliance 85% → +12.4 pts". Banker aur MSME dono samajh sakte hain exactly kyon score aisa aaya. No black box.

SHAP TreeExplainer · per-feature attribution
5

Loan Offers + Recommendations

Score ke hisab se eligible loan products show hote hain — amount, interest rate, EMI calculation. NTC/NTB MSMEs ke liye MUDRA Shishu/Kishore/Tarun, CGTMSE-backed, Stand-Up India. Score improve karne ke actionable tips.

EMI calc · What-If simulator · Peer benchmarking

🌱 NTC / NTB INCLUSION

Credit-Invisible MSMEs Ko Bhi Score Milta Hai

New-to-Credit (NTC) MSMEs — jinke paas koi credit history nahi — unke liye special 4-pillar scoring mode. Credit pillar suppress hota hai, baaki 4 pillars ke weights redistribute hote hain. NTB (New-to-Bank) detection bhi — years_in_business ≤ 2 ya revenue < ₹3L.

NORMAL MODE (5 pillars)
Cash Flow: 25%  ·  Compliance: 20%
Growth: 20%  ·  Stability: 20%
Credit: 15%
→
NTC MODE (4 pillars — credit suppressed)
Cash Flow: 30%  ·  Compliance: 24%
Growth: 23%  ·  Stability: 23%
Credit: N/A (suppressed)
